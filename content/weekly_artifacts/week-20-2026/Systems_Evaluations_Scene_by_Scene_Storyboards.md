# Systems Evaluations Video Series — Scene-by-Scene Storyboards
**Complete Visual Blueprint for 4-Part Video Production**

---

## Introduction

This document provides frame-by-frame storyboards for all 4 videos in the Systems Evaluations series. Each storyboard includes:
- **Visual Description**: What animator/designer creates
- **Narration**: Exact voiceover text with timing
- **Animation**: Timing, easing, movement
- **Audio**: Background music, sound effects, voiceover level
- **Duration**: Scene length and cumulative video time

**Storyboard Format:**
```
SCENE [#.#]: [Scene Title]
Duration: [X seconds]
Cumulative Time: [X:XX]

VISUAL:
[Detailed description of what appears on screen]

NARRATION:
[Voiceover script with timing cues]

ANIMATION:
[Movement, transitions, timing details]

AUDIO:
- BG Music: [Title, volume level]
- Sound effects: [What sounds, when they occur]
- Voiceover: [Level, EQ notes]
```

## SCENE 1.5: MEASURE Framework Introduction
**Duration: 4 seconds**
**Cumulative Time: 0:18-0:22**

**VISUAL:**
- Background darkens slightly (supports focus on central element)
- Navy circular diagram appears center: "MEASURE Loop" (Navy circle, 250px diameter)
- Circle has 5 distinct segments (like a pie chart, but segmented):
  - Segment 1 (0-72°): Label "MEASURE" (Teal text, 18px)
  - Segment 2 (72-144°): Label "EVALUATE" (Teal text, 18px)
  - Segment 3 (144-216°): Label "ASSESS" (Teal text, 18px)
  - Segment 4 (216-288°): Label "SUSTAIN" (Teal text, 18px)
  - Segment 5 (288-360°): Label "IMPROVE" (Teal text, 18px)

- Center of circle: "MEASURE Loop" text (28px, White, Bold, Navy circle behind it)

**NARRATION:**
"This process has a name: the MEASURE loop. It's simple but powerful. M for Measure—collect your data. E for Evaluate—what does the data mean? A for Assess—is that good or bad? S for Sustain—keep what's working. I for Improve—and change what isn't. Then you loop back to M and measure again. The cycle continues."

**ANIMATION:**
- Navy circle appears: Scale 0→1 (0-0.8s, ease-out-back)
- Segments light up sequentially in Teal (one per letter):
  - M lights up: 1s (100ms duration, highlight)
  - E lights up: 1.2s (100ms duration)
  - A lights up: 1.4s (100ms duration)
  - S lights up: 1.6s (100ms duration)
  - I lights up: 1.8s (100ms duration)
- Circular arrow begins rotating: 2s mark (4s full rotation, linear, infinite loop from 2s-4s)
- Center text fades in: 2.5s (300ms fade-in)

**AUDIO:**
- BG Music: Slightly rhythmic, matching the loop concept (0.4 volume, steady beat)
- Voiceover: Clear, methodical, emphasis on each letter as it's introduced
- SFX: Subtle "pulse" sound effect (150ms) each time segment highlights (5 pulses total)
