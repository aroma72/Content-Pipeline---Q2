# Path to Agentic Mastery — Session 01: Course Overview
## Voiceover Script (for approval before generation)

**Target Duration:** 2 minutes (120 seconds)  
**Tone:** Warm, authoritative, inspiring, accessible  
**Audience:** Developers/builders wanting to master AI agents

## SCRIPT (Read-through time: ~2 minutes)

### [0:00-0:15] OPENING - What is This Course?

"Welcome to Path to Agentic Mastery. Over the next weeks, we're building something most teams never get right: AI agents that actually work.

Not toy chatbots. Real, production-grade systems that handle complexity, remember context, and scale."

**[Visual: Google Veo animation - person entering a new space, seeing possibilities]**

---

### [0:15-0:35] THE PROBLEM - Why You Need This

"Right now, most teams are stuck in fire-fighting mode. Patching problems as they come. No system. No structure.

Meanwhile, the teams that win are building systems. They've figured out how to make AI remember, how to chain actions together, how to deploy with confidence.

This course teaches you their playbook."

**[Visual: Remotion animation - split screen showing chaos vs order]**

---

### [0:35-0:55] THE FIVE DOMAINS - What We're Building

"Agentic mastery has five critical domains:

**One:** Mental Models — How context windows work, why AI has the goldfish problem, and how to fix it.

**Two:** Memory Architecture — Making your AI actually remember across conversations. CLAUDE.md, memory.md, the beads system.

**Three:** Building Skills — Writing modular, reusable skill files. The difference between a skill and an agent.

**Four:** Real World Systems — Databases, APIs, MCPs. How to connect your AI to the world.

**Five:** Advanced Patterns — Evals, testing frameworks, deployment. How to know your system works before users find bugs."

**[Visual: Google Veo animation - 5 domains appearing, connecting, showing their relationship]**

---

### [0:55-1:25] THE JOURNEY - How We'll Get There

"Here's how this works:

**Week 1:** You'll set up Claude, Cursor, and Git. Create your first repo. Learn consumer vs producer mindset. Recognize the 90-10 rule: 90% of problems come from 10% of causes.

**Week 2-3:** You'll build memory architecture. Write your first CLAUDE.md. Understand why foundational files matter.

**Week 4-5:** You'll connect to databases, learn deployment, understand the three-layer architecture that keeps systems alive.

**Week 6+:** You'll sharpen your skills with evals, testing frameworks, and real-world patterns.

By the end, you won't just understand AI agents. You'll know how to build them."

**[Visual: Remotion animation - timeline/journey showing progression, building blocks stacking]**

---

### [1:25-1:40] THE PROMISE - Why It Matters

"This isn't theoretical. Every pattern, every tool, every framework in this course comes from building real products.

You'll go from 'I built a chatbot' to 'I built a system that ships.'"

**[Visual: Google Veo animation - person at computer, confident, surrounded by connected nodes/systems]**

---

### [1:40-2:00] CLOSING - The Invitation

"The difference between a builder and a system builder is mastery.

This course teaches you mastery.

Let's start."

**[Visual: Remotion fade to course logo/title card]**
