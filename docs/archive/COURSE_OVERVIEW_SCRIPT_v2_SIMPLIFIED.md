# Path to Agentic Mastery — Session 01: Course Overview
## Voiceover Script (SIMPLIFIED FOR BEGINNERS)

**Target Duration:** 2 minutes (120 seconds)  
**Tone:** Friendly, encouraging, like a trusted mentor  
**Audience:** Anyone new to AI — no tech experience assumed

---

## SCRIPT (Read-through time: ~2 minutes)

### [0:00-0:15] OPENING - What is This Course?

"Hi. Welcome to Agentic Mastery.

Over the next few weeks, we're going to learn how to build AI assistants that actually work — not just fun chatbots, but real helpers that can do actual work for you and your team."

**[Visual: Google Veo animation - person entering a new space, seeing possibilities]**

---

### [0:15-0:35] THE PROBLEM - Why You Need This

"Think about your team right now. How much time do you spend fixing problems as they come up? Putting out fires, instead of building something solid that works on its own?

Here's the difference: Some teams are always firefighting. Others build systems that just work.

This course teaches you how to be the second team."

**[Visual: Remotion animation - split screen showing chaos vs calm, organized workflow]**

---

### [0:35-0:55] THE FIVE THINGS YOU'LL LEARN

"Agentic mastery has five essential parts:

**One:** How AI thinks — Understanding how AI sees the world. The biggest mistake? Thinking AI remembers things it doesn't.

**Two:** Memory — Teaching your AI to actually remember what you told it. Like giving it a notebook that never forgets.

**Three:** Teaching it to help — Building reusable tools your AI can use. Think of it like giving your AI new superpowers.

**Four:** Connecting to the real world — Linking your AI to databases and apps. Making it not just talk, but take action.

**Five:** Making sure it works — Testing your AI so you catch problems before your users do."

**[Visual: Google Veo animation - 5 simple icons appearing, connecting, showing how they work together]**

---

### [0:55-1:25] THE STEP-BY-STEP JOURNEY

"Here's what happens each week:

**Week 1:** You'll set up your tools and understand the producer mindset. Instead of just using AI, you'll start building with it.

**Week 2-3:** You'll teach your AI to remember. You'll create a simple system that keeps track of everything your AI needs to know.

**Week 4-5:** You'll connect your AI to real databases and tools. Your AI goes from talking to actually doing things.

**Week 6+:** You'll test everything. Make sure your system is bulletproof.

By the end? You won't just understand AI. You'll know how to build something that works."

**[Visual: Remotion animation - simple timeline showing 6 weeks, building blocks stacking up, getting more complete each week]**

---

### [1:25-1:40] WHY THIS MATTERS

"Here's the truth: Anyone can chat with an AI. Not everyone can build an AI system that actually works.

That skill? That's gold. That's what separates tinkerers from builders."

**[Visual: Google Veo animation - person building something, feeling accomplished and confident]**

---

### [1:40-2:00] LET'S GO

"Everything in this course is based on real projects we've built.

Not theory. Real, working systems.

You're ready. Let's start."

**[Visual: Remotion fade to title: "Path to Agentic Mastery" with warm, welcoming design]**
