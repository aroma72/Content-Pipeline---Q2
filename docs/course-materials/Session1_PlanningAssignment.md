## Assignment

**Create a Step-by-Step Plan Using Claude**

For this assignment, think of one idea, project, or problem that you want to work on using Claude. It can be related to your work, learning, team processes, content creation, automation, research, or any practical task you want to improve or develop.

Write a detailed paragraph-length prompt for Claude in which you clearly explain your idea. Include enough context so Claude understands what you want to achieve, why it matters, who it is for, and what kind of outcome you are expecting.

Then, ask Claude to break your idea down into a detailed step-by-step plan. The plan should include clear stages, specific actions, expected outputs, and any important considerations.

Finally, ask Claude to save this plan in a file named `planning.md`.

---

## Your Prompt Should Include:

1. **The idea or project** you want to work on
2. **The purpose or goal** behind the idea
3. **Any relevant context or background** information
4. **The audience or users** involved
5. **The kind of output** you want Claude to create
6. **A clear instruction** to create a step-by-step detailed plan
7. **A clear instruction** to save the final plan as `planning.md`

---

## Example Instruction

Include language like this in your prompt:

> "Please break this idea into a detailed step-by-step implementation plan and save the final output in a file named planning.md."

---

## What to Submit

- Your completed `planning.md` file containing:
  - Clear problem statement or idea definition
  - Defined goals and success criteria
  - Step-by-step implementation plan with stages
  - Specific actions for each stage
  - Expected outputs at each phase
  - Important considerations and risks
  - Timeline or sequencing information
  - Resource requirements (if applicable)

---

## Grading Criteria

Your assignment will be evaluated on:

✅ **Clarity of Idea** — Is the problem/project clearly defined?  
✅ **Completeness of Plan** — Does it include all necessary stages and actions?  
✅ **Practicality** — Is the plan realistic and actionable?  
✅ **Detail Level** — Are steps specific enough to follow?  
✅ **Output Quality** — Is the plan well-organized and easy to understand?  
✅ **File Format** — Is the plan saved correctly as `planning.md`?

---

## Tips for Success

- **Be specific:** The more detail you provide Claude about your idea, the better the plan will be
- **Include context:** Help Claude understand why this matters and who benefits
- **Define success:** Be clear about what "done" looks like
- **Think practically:** Consider resources, timeline, and potential obstacles
- **Ask for clarity:** If Claude's plan is unclear, ask follow-up questions and have them refine it
