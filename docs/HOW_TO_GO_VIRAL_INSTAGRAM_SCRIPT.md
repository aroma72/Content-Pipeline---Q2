# Script — "How to Go Viral on Instagram" (6-second reel)

**Format:** Instagram Reel, vertical 1080×1920, 30fps
**Hard cap:** 6.0 seconds (180 frames)
**Pacing target:** energetic reel pace ~2.5–2.8 words/sec (clear, not rushed)

## Voiceover (16 words)

> Want to go viral? Win the first second. Hook them before they scroll — that's the game.

**Word budget check:** 16 words ÷ ~5.5 s speaking span ≈ 2.9 words/sec → under the 3.0 wps rushed ceiling (revised down from 17 words after Reviewer 2 flagged 3.08 wps).

## Single idea

One actionable tip only (a 6s reel can land exactly one): **front-load your hook — earn the first second or you lose the viewer.**

## Scene plan (4 fast cuts — reel-style, anti-boring)

| Scene | Time | Frames | On-screen text | VO line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–1.3s | 0–39 | "WANT TO GO VIRAL?" | "Want to go viral?" |
| 2 | 1.3–3.0s | 39–90 | "WIN THE FIRST SECOND" | "Win the first second." |
| 3 | 3.0–4.6s | 90–138 | "HOOK THEM ↗ BEFORE THEY SCROLL" | "Hook them before they scroll —" |
| 4 | 4.6–6.0s | 138–180 | "THAT'S THE WHOLE GAME 🔥" | "that's the whole game." |

4 scenes / 6s = ~1.5s average per cut → fast enough to feel like a reel, not a slideshow.

## Brand / visual direction

- High-contrast, bold sans-serif, large headline (per "headings 4+ sizes larger than body").
- Energetic accent color + motion (scale/slide on each cut) to avoid static = boring.
- No "mentor tone" or stage directions rendered on screen.
