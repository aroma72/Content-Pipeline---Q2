## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS FROM SERIES

### Testing vs Evaluation
- **Testing**: "Does it work?"
- **Evaluation**: "Is it right?"

### The MEASURE Framework
1. **M**etrics — What are you measuring?
2. **E**quity — Who benefits? Who is harmed?
3. **A**ccuracy — How reliable is it?
4. **S**afety — What are failure modes?
5. **U**sability — Can people actually use it?
6. **R**eliability — Does it work consistently?
7. **E**thics — Is it the right thing to do?

### The Core Message
> "A system can pass all tests and still be profoundly wrong."

Evaluation isn't optional. It's essential.
