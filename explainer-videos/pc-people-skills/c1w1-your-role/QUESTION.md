# The question for c1w1-your-role

This question is **not in the video**. The LMS pauses the video at the point below, shows it as a
pop-up, the learner must pick an answer to continue, sees the feedback, and the video resumes.
The LMS developer reads it from `checkpoint.json` in the repo. To change anything here, tell Claude:
the edit goes into the checkpoint in beats.js and both files are regenerated.

## Question 1 — pauses at 1:38 of the final video

*After the line in beat 16; resumes on beat 18.*

**A new joiner tells you they feel lost in their team. Which response supports them rather than rescues them?**

A. Ask to move them to a different team straight away
B. Ask what feels hardest, then help them plan one first step  ✅ *correct*
C. Tell them everyone feels this way and it will pass
D. Promise them their manager will sort it out by Friday

**If they choose the right answer, they see:**

> Exactly right. Asking what feels hardest keeps the problem theirs and shows you are listening, and planning one first step together helps them act. Next time they will have done it once already.

**If they choose a wrong answer, they see:**

> The supporting response is to ask what feels hardest and help them plan one first step. Moving them to a new team is rescuing: you solve the problem for them, and they learn nothing about settling in. Saying everyone feels this way dismisses what they told you, so they stop telling you things. Promising the manager will fix it by Friday is a promise you cannot keep, and it moves the problem onto someone who is not in the room.
