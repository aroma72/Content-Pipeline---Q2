# The question for c1w2-beyond-the-reaction

This question is **not in the video**. The LMS pauses the video at the point below, shows it as a
pop-up, the learner must pick an answer to continue, sees the feedback, and the video resumes.
The LMS developer reads it from `checkpoint.json` in the repo. To change anything here, tell Claude:
the edit goes into the checkpoint in beats.js and both files are regenerated.

## Question 1 — pauses at 1:45 of the final video

*After the line in beat 15; resumes on beat 17.*

**A senior employee has gone quiet since a restructure moved their team under a newer manager. What do you do first?**

A. Explain the business reasons for the restructure to them again
B. Ask them privately how the change lands, and ask for their read on the new setup  ✅ *correct*
C. Leave it for now; they will adjust in a few weeks
D. Tell them in the team meeting that their role matters as much as ever

**If they choose the right answer, they see:**

> Exactly. The quiet is the tip of the iceberg; underneath is status. Asking privately, and asking for their expert read, gives back the standing the change took away.

**If they choose a wrong answer, they see:**

> The need underneath is status, so you respond to that: ask privately how it lands, and ask for their read on the new setup, which shows their experience still counts. Explaining the business reasons again answers certainty, not status, and can feel like a lecture. Leaving it lets the silence harden into disengagement. Reassuring them in the team meeting puts them on the spot in front of others, which can make the status threat worse, and it tells rather than shows.
