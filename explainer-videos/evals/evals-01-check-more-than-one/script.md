# Why You Can't Just Eyeball It — draft

**Teaches:** why checking AI output by eye does not scale. The same request comes back different, and good is a range, not a yes or no.
**One move:** check more than one, and count.
**Everyday picture:** a crate of mangoes. One taste, then ten tastes and a tally.

Deferred: error analysis (video 2), naming it (video 3), writing criteria (video 5), judges (video 6).

## Script (one beat per line · [ali] = character · [info] = infographic)

1.  [info] Check more than one answer, and count how many are good.
2.  [ali]  Ali learned this the hard way at his fruit stall.
3.  [ali]  A crate of a hundred mangoes arrives on Tuesday morning.
4.  [ali]  He takes one from the top, tastes it, and it is sweet.
5.  [ali]  So he wheels the whole crate out and starts selling.
6.  [info] One mango tasted. A hundred mangoes sold.
7.  [ali]  By evening, three customers come back with sour mangoes.
8.  [ali]  Ali cannot understand it, because his one was lovely.
9.  [info] At the bottom of the crate, the mangoes are hard and sour.
10. [ali]  Every mango in the crate is a little bit different.
11. [ali]  His one mango only told him about that one mango.
12. [info] Some are honey. Some are fine. Some make you wince.
13. [ali]  Next Tuesday the same crate arrives, and Ali does it differently.
14. [ali]  He takes ten mangoes from the top, the middle and the bottom.
15. [ali]  He tastes each one and marks it on a scrap of paper.
16. [info] Seven sweet. Three sour. *(caption: this is an eval)*
17. [ali]  Now Ali knows something about the whole crate.
18. [info] Seven last week. Four this week. Time to call the supplier.
19. [ali]  It works the same way when Ali asks AI to write his price list.
20. [ali]  He asks once, reads it, and it looks fine.
21. [info] Ask the same thing again. The answer comes back different.
22. [ali]  It is not wrong. It is just different, like the next mango.
23. [ali]  One answer that looks fine is one mango off the top.
24. [ali]  Today, ask AI for the same thing ten times.
25. [ali]  Mark each answer good or bad, and count them.
26. [info] Or say this to Claude: "Run this prompt ten times, show me the answers, and count how many are good." *(spoken in full; same words on a card)*
27. [ali]  Ten answers and a number beat one glance.

## Gate check

**READY** · 27 beats · 18 [ali] / 9 [info] · average 10 words per line

- **One move:** check ten and count. No jargon spoken.
- **Everyday picture:** the crate, shown from beat 3 to beat 23.
- **Before → after:** one tasted and a hundred sold (6), three customers back (7). Then ten tasted from three depths and a tally (14–16).
- **Character:** takes the shortcut, gets caught by evening, digs in, changes what he does, gets a number.
- **Close:** do it today (24–25) plus a prompt card (26).

**Analogy limit, on purpose.** Mangoes differ because they are different mangoes. AI answers differ because the model is random. The mechanism is not the same, but the lesson is: one sample tells you nothing about the next. Beats 19 to 22 say the AI version in plain words so the technical fact sits next to the picture.
