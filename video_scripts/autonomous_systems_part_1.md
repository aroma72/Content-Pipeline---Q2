## KEY CONCEPTS TO COVER IN VOICEOVER

1. **Consumer Mindset Definition**
   - Asking the system to do specific tasks
   - Giving step-by-step instructions
   - High human dependency

2. **Producer Mindset Definition**
   - Setting goals, not instructions
   - System figures out execution
   - Low human dependency

3. **Autonomy Concept**
   - The core difference between both
   - Reduced human intervention
   - System takes ownership and responsibility
   - Self-managing capabilities
