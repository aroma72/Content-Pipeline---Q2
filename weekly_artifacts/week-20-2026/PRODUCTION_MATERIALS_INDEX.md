### 4. Voiceover Coaching Guide
**File:** `Systems_Evaluations_Voiceover_Coaching_Guide.md` (80+ pages)
**Purpose:** Comprehensive narration direction for voice talent

**Contents:**
- Overall tone and character ("Wise Coach")
- Pacing framework (Slow/Deliberate 90-110 WPM, Conversational 130-150 WPM, Energized 150-170 WPM)
- Vocal characteristics (pitch, inflection, energy, breathing)
- Scene-by-scene coaching (Video 1 fully detailed, Videos 2-4 summarized)
- Technical recording specifications (48kHz, 24-bit, clean audio)
- Pronunciation guide for technical terms
- Emotional arc for each video
- Performance tips and quality standards
- Common mistakes to avoid
- Preparation checklist and collaboration guide

**Key Guidance:**
- Conversational but professional tone (not academic, not hyper-casual)
- Strategic pacing variation (slower for concepts, faster for examples)
- Emphasis through pitch change, not volume change
- Natural breathing at sentence breaks
- 0.5-1 second pauses after key statements

**Recording Standards:**
- 48kHz, 24-bit, WAV format
- Peak levels: -6dB to -3dB
- Clean environment, no background noise
- Light EQ and compression applied post-production

**Who Uses It:** Voice talent (primary), voiceover director, recording engineer, editor
