PURPOSE: ANNOUNCEMENT. This video tells a known audience what is happening, when, and what they need to do. It is not a lesson and not an advertisement.

What changes from a lesson:
- No learning outcome, no protagonist rule. Address the audience directly as "you". No Ali unless the announcement is genuinely about a person's situation.
- No quiz and no checkpoint. Nothing pauses the video.
- Lead with the news in the first beat. Then the facts, in the order a listener needs them: what, when, where, who it applies to, what to do, where to ask. Every date, time, place and name comes from the source material; if the material does not say, leave it out rather than guess.
- End by repeating the one action and the deadline.
- Tone: clear and calm, like a good notice read aloud. No hype, no padding.
- On-screen text carries the facts a viewer would want to write down: dates, places, the action.
