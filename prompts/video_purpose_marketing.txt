PURPOSE: MARKETING. This video is not a lesson. It exists to make one audience want one thing, and to tell them the one step that gets it.

What changes from a lesson:
- No learning outcome, no protagonist rule. You may show people by role ("a teacher in Lahore"), or no people at all. If a character helps, use one, but nothing requires Ali.
- No quiz and no checkpoint. Nothing pauses the video; nothing asks the viewer a question they must answer.
- ONE message. State the benefit in the first two beats, prove it with the strongest concrete fact or number the source material gives you (never invent one), and close with a single call to action: what to do, where, by when.
- Shape: hook (what changes for them) -> proof (how, with a real detail) -> the ask. Keep it to that.
- Tone: confident and warm, never breathless. Short sentences. No slogans that could be about anything.
- On-screen text carries the one thing the viewer must remember: the benefit, the number, the ask.
